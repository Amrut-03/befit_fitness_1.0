BeFit Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the BeFit app ("BeFit", "we") collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. It applies to the BeFit apps for Android and iOS.

2. Health Connect and Apple Health

If you choose to connect, BeFit asks for read-only access to the following data in Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS):

  • Steps and active calories burned: shown in the Home activity rings and used to track your daily goals.
  • Heart rate, sleep and weight: shown in the 7-day and 30-day charts.

BeFit never writes to, changes or deletes anything in Health Connect or Apple Health.

These readings are read live and shown on your device. BeFit does not store Health Connect or Apple Health readings on its servers, does not save them to its on-device database, and does not use them for advertising. The only exception is the daily insights feature described in section 4.

You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect's settings (Android) or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → BeFit (iOS). If you don't connect, or you deny access, BeFit falls back to your phone's pedometer to count steps.

BeFit's use of data received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from Apple Health is not shared with third parties for advertising or data-brokering purposes, and is not used for anything other than the health and fitness features described here.

3. Data collected automatically

  • App interactions (for example: signed in, workout saved, meal completed, barcode scanned), through Firebase Analytics, to understand how the app is used.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics, through Firebase Crashlytics, in release builds only, to fix bugs.
  • Device identifiers: the Firebase Analytics app-instance ID and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, for analytics and to send notifications.

The camera is used only to scan barcodes on your device. Progress cards you create are saved on your device and are never uploaded. BeFit does not collect your location.

4. Daily insights (Google Gemini)

To generate your daily insights, BeFit sends today's step count, calories burned, your workout streak and your BMI to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic. When Health Connect or Apple Health is connected, the steps and calories come from there. No name, email or account ID is included in this request. Google processes it as our service provider to return the insights, under the Firebase data processing terms. The generated insight text is cached on your device.

5. Service providers

BeFit is built on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging and AI Logic). Google processes data on our behalf only to provide these services. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone for advertising.

6. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Your data in Cloud Firestore can only be read or written by your own signed-in account.

7. Retention and deletion

Account and profile data, workouts, meals and manual metrics are kept until you delete your account. To delete your account, open Profile → Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes your Firestore data and your sign-in account, revokes BeFit's Google access and clears the data stored on your device. Analytics and crash data are kept according to Firebase's standard retention periods.

If you no longer have the app, email us at the address below and we will delete your account and data.

8. Children

BeFit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
